How It Works

Inside a GrowMate® project.

What a project looks like, who your child works with, and how you see the growth.

Example project · November

After Halloween

A thinking project. Children look at a familiar holiday from different perspectives.

They run a survey and do research to form their own views, turn those views into work they can present, and share it with peers and families at the Showcase.

  1. Do people from different backgrounds all celebrate Halloween?
  2. Should people dress up as monsters and ghosts?
  3. Why are we scared in a haunted house we know is fake?

Small groups. Active participation.

Every child has a role.

Peer groups

About four same-age children work on the same project. Each one shares ideas, takes on responsibilities and gives feedback, so nobody sits on the sidelines.

Mentors

Mentors spark children’s thinking, guide group discussion and encourage children to work together. They also observe and record each child’s HEART™ competencies in action.

Between sessions

Each week your child completes a short Check-in with a photo of their progress. An optional Challenge at home lets them go further, and you can add your own observations before it is submitted.

Interdisciplinary projects

Projects drawn from eight domains.

Each project connects several domains, so children use what they learn at school in new and real situations.

Growth you can see

What you will see, and when.

  • Every week

    Progress at home

    Check-in progress and mentor comments in your child’s GrowMate® profile.

  • Every month

    The Showcase

    Watch your child present finished work and answer questions in person.

  • Over time

    HEART™ Growth Report

    Specific examples of what your child did, where they are strong and what to practise next.

  • At school

    Stronger performance

    In class discussions, group work, presentations and inquiry assignments.

→

Growth Drops → Growth badges

Children earn Growth Drops through weekly Check-ins and the Showcase Reflect. As drops add up, they unlock Growth badges and their Growth Tree grows.

→

Challenge Stars → Explorer badges

Optional Challenges earn Challenge Stars. As stars add up, they unlock Explorer badges, for children who choose to go further.

HEART™ badges

Nominated by mentors when a child shows clear growth in one of the five HEART™ competencies.

Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions.

What does my child need for the online sessions?

A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone, and a quiet place to work. We share session times when you apply.

Where is the Showcase?

The Showcase is held in person once a month, and families are invited. We share the venue details with enrolled families.

Do I need to help with the project?

No. You don’t need to take part in the project or drive your child anywhere. Just provide a quiet space and the right device. When your child chooses to do a Challenge, you act as a witness and add a short observation.

Is GrowMate® a tutoring program?

No. GrowMate® is not subject tutoring, but it complements school education. Children strengthen their core competencies and subject skills, which supports how they perform at school and can be reflected in their marks over time.

Does GrowMate® teach AI tools?

No, we don’t teach AI tools. Children practise asking good questions, thinking critically and making sound judgments: the skills they will need to direct AI in the future.

How much does it cost?

Scan the WeChat QR code at the bottom of this page and a member of our team will contact you.

Can my child join or leave partway through the year?

Yes. GrowMate® runs month by month, so your child can join at the start of a month or leave at the end of one.

Can siblings join together?

Yes, and we offer a sibling discount.

What if my child misses a session?

We can arrange a make-up session. A fee may apply.
