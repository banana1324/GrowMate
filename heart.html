About HEART™

Five Human Core Competencies for the AI era.

HEART™ is GrowMate™’s framework for how children collaborate, communicate, act, adapt, and think.

Why These Five

A practical framework for human development

HEART™ draws on the OECD Learning Compass 2030 and the P21 Framework for 21st Century Learning. Rather than simply adopting either framework, GrowMate™ organizes their broader ideas into five areas that children can practice and mentors can observe through real projects.

Together, the five Human Core Competencies cover how a child works with others, communicates ideas, turns thinking into action, manages themselves through difficulty, and thinks critically and creatively.

As AI takes on more academic and technical tasks, these human capabilities become increasingly important in how children learn, work, adapt, and make decisions.

Each competency is broken into six development indicators, giving HEART™ 30 observable indicators in total. This turns broad ideas like “collaboration” or “resilience” into specific behaviors that can be noticed, discussed, and tracked over time.

HEART™

The Five Human Core Competencies

H

Human Collaboration

Build relationships and move forward with others.

Human Collaboration is the ability to build connections, take on a role, understand other people, navigate disagreement, share responsibility, and help a group make progress.

In the AI era: AI can organize information and support teamwork, but trust, empathy, conflict navigation, and shared responsibility remain deeply human.

E

Expression & Communication

Turn ideas into understanding.

Expression & Communication is the ability to express ideas clearly, understand others, ask for clarification, respond thoughtfully, and adapt communication to different people and situations.

In the AI era: AI can generate and polish language, but children still need their own point of view, judgment, and the ability to connect with a real audience.

A

Action & Problem Solving

Turn thinking into action.

Action & Problem Solving is the ability to identify a problem, take initiative, make a plan, follow through, adjust when things change, and take ownership of the outcome.

In the AI era: AI can generate options and analyze possibilities. Children still need to decide what matters, act on it, and take responsibility for what happens next.

R

Resilience & Self-management

Stay steady, adapt, and keep moving.

Resilience & Self-management is the ability to regulate emotions, stay focused through difficulty, recover from setbacks, and gradually manage work and responsibility with greater independence.

In the AI era: AI can make things easier. It cannot build resilience for you.

T

Thinking & Creativity

Ask better questions and create better possibilities.

Thinking & Creativity is the ability to ask meaningful questions, reason from evidence, test assumptions, shift perspectives, generate original ideas, and combine different ideas into something new.

In the AI era: AI can generate answers and ideas at extraordinary speed. Children still need to decide what to ask, what to trust, what matters, and where to go next.
