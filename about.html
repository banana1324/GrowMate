About Us

Every child deserves to grow up capable,
not just for school, but for a life of their own.

GrowMate™ was built around a simple question: what abilities will children keep using when the tools around them keep changing?

Why We're Here

The world our children are growing into is changing quickly.

As AI makes many academic and technical tasks easier, those skills alone are no longer enough to set a child apart.

What matters more in the AI era is the ability to work with people, communicate ideas, make decisions, recover from setbacks, and think critically and creatively.

These abilities cannot be built through instruction alone. They grow through experience, challenge, and reflection.

GrowMate™ was built to create that practice through real projects, small peer groups, and Role Model Mentors.

What We Believe

Five beliefs that guide GrowMate™

01

Ability grows through challenge

Human Core Competencies cannot be built through instruction alone. They develop when children have real work to do and real people to work with.

02

Learning how to learn lasts a lifetime

GrowLoop™ gives children a repeatable way to approach unfamiliar problems: inquire, design, create, revise, present, and reflect. This way of learning can travel across subjects, projects, and new situations.

03

Long-term capability matters

Grades show part of where a child is today. Capability shapes how far they can go. GrowMate™ focuses on the habits and abilities children will keep using as the world around them changes.

04

Human capability should lead AI

Children should learn to use AI without becoming dependent on it. They need the judgment to question its output, decide when it helps, and use it to turn their own ideas into action.

05

Parents matter

Parents do not need to manage the project. Their role is to notice, encourage, and give children room to struggle, recover, and take ownership.

Vision

To become a trusted platform for Human Core Competencies development, helping young people prepare for an AI-driven world.

Mission

To help young people systematically build Human Core Competencies through real projects, real relationships, and real challenges.
