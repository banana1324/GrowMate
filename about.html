About GrowMate®

Growth is never a solo journey.

GrowMate® is a human skills education system that develops essential human competencies in children and teens through real-world project-based learning.

Our story

Why GrowMate® began.

After 20 years of experience in university, secondary and early-childhood education, and as a mother of two, I kept noticing the same thing. The children who thrive are not only the ones with the best marks. They are the ones who ask good questions, work well with others, speak up and keep going when things get hard.

Schools value these skills, and the AI era will need them even more. Yet a busy classroom rarely has time to practise them. I spent a year and a half building GrowMate® to give children that practice, and to help parents see the growth.

Sissi YuanFounder, GrowMate®

Mission & vision

What we work toward.

Our mission

To help children and teens develop essential human skills through structured, real-world project-based learning.

Our vision

A future where young people have the human skills to learn independently, think critically, collaborate and shape their futures.

MASTER AI.STAY HUMAN.

What our slogan means

The stronger AI becomes, the more human skills matter. When children build the five HEART™ competencies, they gain the confidence to direct AI rather than be replaced by it.

GrowMate® and school

We work alongside school, not instead of it.

  • Complements school

    We give focused, regular practice in the competencies schools value, organised into five HEART™ areas: Human Collaboration, Expression & Communication, Action & Problem Solving, Resilience & Self-management, and Thinking & Creativity.

  • Not subject tutoring

    We don’t copy each subject’s classroom pace. Children use what they learn at school in interdisciplinary, real-world projects.

  • Growth you can see

    We don’t promise test scores. You see real progress: finished work at every Showcase, HEART™ Growth Reports, and more and more confidence in how your child performs at school.

Why “GrowMate”

  • Peers who grow alongside them

  • Parents who support them
  • GrowMate®, a mate for parents too
