The Problem We Solve

Technology changes fast. Human ability matters for longer.

AI tools will keep changing. The technical skill that feels urgent today may look very different in a few years.

What lasts longer is a child's ability to think clearly, work with other people, communicate an idea, act on a plan, and recover when something does not work.

GrowMate™ gives children repeated practice with those abilities through real projects and real interaction.

The HEART™ Framework

Five Human Core Competencies

HEART™ gives us a simple way to name, observe, and discuss the abilities children are practicing.

H

Human Collaboration

Build trust, take a role, listen to others, work through disagreement, and help a group move forward.

AI can support a team. It cannot build human trust for you.

E

Expression & Communication

Explain an idea clearly, understand other people, ask for clarification, and adapt to the audience.

AI can polish words. A child still needs a point of view and a voice.

A

Action & Problem Solving

Define the problem, make a plan, start, adjust when needed, and take responsibility for the result.

AI can suggest options. People still have to decide and act.

R

Resilience & Self-management

Manage emotions, stay steady under pressure, recover from setbacks, and build better habits.

A reminder app can prompt you. It cannot do the hard part of recovering for you.

T

Thinking & Creativity

Ask useful questions, judge evidence, shift perspective, and combine ideas in new ways.

AI can produce answers. Children still need to judge what is worth asking and trusting.

Five competencies · 30 development indicators · observed through real behavior, not a test score

How It Works

1

2

3

4

Three Pillars

What the program is built around

🔨

Real Projects

Each month begins with a real driving question. Children investigate, make choices, build something, revise it, present it, and reflect.

👥

Peer Groups

Each child works with a small group of same-age peers. They share progress, give feedback, and learn how to work with other people.

🧭

Role Model Mentors

Mentors guide the process without doing the work for the child. They ask questions, notice behavior, and help children reflect.
